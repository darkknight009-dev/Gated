# Twitter Posts — Gated Build in Public

One post per day. Relatable. Short. Psychological hooks.

---

### Day 1 — The Problem
```
I built an AI that reads your inbox.

Not to reply. Not to summarize.
Just to answer one question:

"Does this email deserve my attention right now?"

Gated. Coming soon.
```
**Hook:** Curiosity gap — no explanation of "how." People DM to ask.

---

### Day 2 — The Relatable Pain
```
Your inbox has 1,247 unread emails.

None of them are urgent.
All of them feel urgent.

I'm building something that ranks them by what actually matters to YOU — not by who sends the most.

Gated.
```
**Hook:** Universal pain. Everyone has felt this. Shareable = "this is me."

---

### Day 3 — The Confession
```
Hot take:

Most inbox zero apps are productivity porn.
You open them. You feel good. You go back to 1,000 unread.

Gated doesn't aim for inbox zero.
It aims for "I only opened the 3 emails that mattered."
```
**Hook:** Contrarian take on a popular concept. People love defending their opinions → engagement.

---

### Day 4 — The Behind-the-Scenes
```
Spent 6 hours debugging why AI scores were inverted.

Turns out: the model thought newsletters were "IMPORTANT."

My deterministic engine said "newsletter? no."
The AI said "high relevance."

Both were right.
I just needed to let them argue it out.
```
**Hook:** Real debugging story. Engineers love this. Non-engineers love the drama.

---

### Day 5 — The Psychological Reframe
```
Your email app gives you everything.
Gated gives you only what matters.

That's not a feature.
That's the difference between overwhelm and clarity.

Less is more. But only if the "less" is right.

Gated.
```
**Hook:** Reframes "less features" as intentional design, not limitation. Aspirational.

---

### Day 6 — The Funny One
```
My AI assistant and my deterministic engine got into an argument about whether a newsletter is important.

The AI said: "Definitely."
The rules said: "Absolutely not."

I made them vote.
Deterministic won 7-1.

Democracy works for email too.
```
**Hook:** Humor + technical inside. Meme potential.

### Day 7 — The Social Proof Build
```
Day 7 of building Gated in public.

Things I learned:
→ Clean code doesn't mean clean thinking
→ My best ideas came at 2am, not during "deep work hours"
→ The hardest part isn't AI. It's deciding what to do with the output.

One week more. Then I show you the actual thing.
```

**Hook:** Build-in-public accountability + vulnerability. Followers want to see the finished product.

### Day 10 — The Specific Win
```
Just shipped this:

An email from your bank about "unusual activity" gets flagged IMPORTANT.
An email from Netflix saying "we miss you" gets silently de-emphasized.

No settings. No training. Just rules.

That's the Attention Score working.
```
**Hook:** Concrete before/after example. Readers instantly imagine themselves using it.

---

### Day 14 — The Philosophical
```
I used to think AI would rank my email for me.

Now I think AI should only assist the ranking.

Because I don't want a machine deciding what I care about.
I want a machine helping me confirm what I already care about.

That line changes everything about how Gated works.
```
**Hook:** Unexpected philosophy. Stands out from "shipped X" posts.

---

## Posting Tips

| Principle | Why |
|---|---|
| **Post at 8-10am or 6-9pm** | Peak Twitter engagement windows |
| **Always include a thread** | Single tweets underperform; threads compound |
| **End with a question or CTA** | Replies boost algorithmic reach |
| **Show real screenshots** | Build-in-public with code/UI screenshots gets 3x engagement |
| **Admit struggles publicly** | Vulnerability creates trust and following |
| **Use the "GATED" reply hook** | Creates a tracking mechanism for your audience |
